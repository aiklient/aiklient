## AiKlient: booking bot for Telegram and WhatsApp

A booking app for nail techs, hairdressers, brow and lash artists and small salons.
Clients book themselves 24/7 in Telegram or WhatsApp and see only your free slots. You confirm each booking with one tap.

- Reminders a day before and 2 hours before the visit
- AI answers client questions using your own prices, hours and address
- Free business page with online booking
- Google Calendar sync
- 8 languages
